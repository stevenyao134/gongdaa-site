How Ordering Works

From First Inquiry to Container in Four Steps

Quotes come back within 10 hours during China business hours, and samples ship free, so you can judge the surface in your own light before committing to a production run.

1. Send your requirement

Tell us the product line, a colour code or reference photo, the panel size, the quantity per colour and your destination port. A short message is enough to start — you do not need a finished specification.

2. Quote in 10 hours

Our sales team replies with price, lead time and shipping options within 10 hours, Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 China time. Free samples follow by DHL, UPS or FedEx for quality checking.

3. Approve the sample

Our standard minimum is 2,000 metres per colour, and smaller quantities can be drawn from stock. With 1,200+ printing rollers and 300+ embossing rollers we colour-match most references without new tooling, under full OEM/ODM protection for your exclusive colourways.

4. Production & shipping

Stock items ship in 5–10 days; production runs take 15–20 days after payment. Rolls leave from Guangzhou or Shenzhen by 20GP or 40HQ sea freight, with air freight for urgent orders, under FOB, CIF or door-to-door DDP terms.

What to include for the fastest quote

Five details are enough for an accurate first price: the product line — decorative film, edge banding, membrane foil or self-adhesive film; the colour reference, whether that is a code, a physical sample or a photo; the dimensions and thickness; the quantity per colour, so we can check stock against production; and your destination port and target market, which decides whether REACH, RoHS or CARB Phase 2 paperwork and an SGS test report travel with the shipment.

Payment is T/T 30% deposit with 70% before delivery, or 100% irrevocable LC at sight. Prefer to talk it through?
