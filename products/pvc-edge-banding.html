Production Process

How Our PVC Edge Banding Is Made

From PVC pellets to packed export cartons — the complete process, in our own factory.

1. Raw Material

PVC compound pellets selected and tested before production.

2. Color Matching

Inks mixed in-house to match any film design or custom color.

3. Extrusion

The compound is extruded into continuous base tape.

4. Calendering

Precision rollers set thickness from 0.4 to 3 mm.

5. Printing & Embossing

Gravure printing and embossing create the decorative surface.

6. Cooling & Cutting

Strips are cooled and slit to the target width.

7. Winding

Tape is wound into clean, uniform rolls.

8. Packing

Rolls are cartoned, labeled and staged for shipment.
