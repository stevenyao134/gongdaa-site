Application

PVC Film for Retail & Hospitality

Premium surface finishes for stores, hotels, restaurants and commercial interiors that demand visual impact and durability.

Where First Impressions Matter

Retail and hospitality interiors operate in the most visually demanding environment in commercial construction. A store's fixtures, a hotel's reception desk, a restaurant's wall paneling — these surfaces must communicate brand identity, withstand heavy foot traffic and frequent cleaning, and remain pristine through years of daily use. Natural materials like solid wood, marble and leather deliver on aesthetics but fall short on maintenance, cost and installation speed.

GONGDA decorative PVC film bridges this gap. Our product range includes luxury textures — brushed metal, Calacatta marble, deep-grain walnut, champagne metallic and custom-brand prints — laminated onto lightweight substrates that install in hours rather than days. The result is a surface that looks and feels premium, costs a fraction of natural material, and requires only routine wiping to maintain.

Why PVC Film for Commercial Spaces?

Rapid Renovation Cycles

Retail brands refresh store concepts every 3–5 years. PVC-laminated fixtures can be resurfaced in place or replaced as modular units — eliminating the demolition, dust and downtime of traditional renovation. A 200m² store refit completes in 5–7 days versus 3–4 weeks.

Heavy-Duty Durability

Commercial-grade PVC film with reinforced topcoat achieves Martindale abrasion > 15,000 cycles and Taber CS-17 < 50mg. Resists shopping cart contact, luggage wheels, luggage rack abrasion and high-frequency cleaning with commercial disinfectants.

Brand-Exact Customization

Custom color matching to brand Pantone specifications. Digital printing capability for logo integration, pattern repeats and brand-specific graphics directly onto the PVC film substrate — no secondary labeling or appliqué required.

Sector-Specific Applications

Retail & Shopfitting

Display fixtures, shelving units, cashier counters, fitting rooms and wall paneling all benefit from PVC film's combination of aesthetics and durability. Fashion retailers favor super-matte and soft-touch finishes that don't compete with merchandise for visual attention. Electronics retailers prefer metallic and carbon-fiber textures that reinforce a tech-forward brand image.

Common specifications: 0.20–0.30mm film on 18mm MDF or aluminum honeycomb substrate. Edge banding 1–2mm with anti-chipping profile. Custom-printed brand graphics on 0.15mm digital-print PVC overlay.

Hotels & Restaurants

Hotel reception desks, room doors, headboards, vanity units and restaurant bar fronts require surfaces that communicate luxury while surviving intensive housekeeping. Marble-texture PVC film on WPC panels achieves the look of stone without the weight, porosity or sealing maintenance of real marble. Wood-grain film on MDF provides the warmth of veneer at a fraction of the cost and with superior moisture resistance.

Fire-rated substrates (B1 grade) meet hotel building codes. Anti-bacterial topcoat options available for healthcare and food-service adjacent applications.
