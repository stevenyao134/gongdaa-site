PVC Film for Wardrobes & Closets

Elegant, tactile surfaces for sliding doors, hinged doors and closet interiors at a fraction of solid-wood cost.

Transforming Closet Manufacturing

Wardrobes and closets are high-visibility furniture pieces where surface aesthetics directly influence purchasing decisions. GONGDA decorative PVC film enables manufacturers to produce premium-looking wardrobe systems — from sliding door panels to interior drawer fronts — using cost-effective MDF and particle-board substrates wrapped with authentic wood grain, marble, metallic or soft-touch velvet finishes.

Unlike traditional veneer or paint, PVC film delivers color consistency across large production batches, zero volatile organic compound (VOC) emissions post-curing, and resistance to the scuffs and scratches common in daily closet use.

Why Manufacturers Choose Our Film

Soft-Touch Velvet Finish

Our super-matte and velvet-touch PVC films provide a warm, fingerprint-resistant surface that elevates the tactile experience of wardrobe doors. Ideal for high-end residential and boutique hotel projects.

Scratch & Scuff Resistance

Topcoat hardening technology achieves pencil hardness 2H–3H. Resists belt buckles, jewelry contact and hanger abrasion — the most common damage sources in closet environments.

OEM Color Matching

Send us your target color sample (Pantone, RAL or physical swatch) and we formulate a color-matched PVC film within 7–10 working days. Consistent batch-to-batch color ΔE < 1.5.

Application Breakdown

Sliding & Hinged Wardrobe Doors

Large-format door panels (up to 2.7m height) are laminated with decorative PVC film on aluminum-frame or MDF-core substrates. Synchronized 3D embossing aligns surface texture with printed wood grain, producing a realistic tactile experience indistinguishable from natural wood at conversational distance.

Popular textures: oak, walnut, ash, marble white, concrete gray, linen and metallic champagne. Thickness: 0.20–0.35mm for flat lamination; 0.35–0.50mm for membrane-pressed profiles.

Interior Components & Drawer Fronts

Drawer fronts, side panels and shelf edges receive color-matched PVC edge banding for a fully coordinated interior. Soft-touch film on drawer fronts creates a premium feel every time a user opens the closet.

Interior lining film (0.12–0.18mm) protects particle-board from moisture in humid climates and simplifies cleaning. Available in white, cream, light wood tones and custom-matched interior colors.
