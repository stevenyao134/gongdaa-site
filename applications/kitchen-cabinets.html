PVC Film for Kitchen Cabinets

Moisture-resistant, oil-proof and easy-clean decorative surfaces for modern kitchen cabinetry.

Why PVC Film for Kitchen Cabinetry?

Kitchen environments demand surfaces that withstand constant exposure to moisture, grease, heat and daily wear. GONGDA decorative PVC film offers a cost-effective, high-performance alternative to solid wood, laminate or painted finishes — delivering authentic wood grain, marble and solid-color aesthetics without the maintenance burden of natural materials.

Our PVC membrane foil is thermoformed onto MDF or particle-board substrates, creating seamless 3D-wrapped cabinet doors with embossed textures that replicate natural material grain patterns. The surface is fully sealed, preventing moisture ingress and making cleaning as simple as wiping with a damp cloth.

Key Performance Advantages

Water & Oil Resistance

Closed-cell PVC surface structure blocks liquid absorption. Resists cooking oil, steam condensation and accidental spills — critical for kitchen longevity.

Heat Tolerance

Formulation withstands intermittent heat exposure up to 60°C without delamination or discoloration. Suitable for cabinet zones near cooktops and ovens.

Stain Resistance

Smooth, non-porous finish prevents food stains and grease buildup. Common household cleaners will not damage the surface — reduces maintenance to a quick wipe.

Typical Application Zones

Cabinet Doors (Base & Wall Units)

Thermoformed PVC membrane foil wraps MDF door profiles with synchronized 3D embossing. Available in matte, super-matte, high-gloss and soft-touch textures. Popular colors include white, gray, walnut, oak and modern two-tone combinations.

Thickness range: 0.18–0.35mm for flat doors; 0.35–0.50mm for deep-drawn 3D profiles.

Carcass Interiors & Shelving

Solid-color PVC self-adhesive film or hot-laminated decorative film lines particle-board carcasses, protecting against moisture ingress from sink zones and steam. Common interior colors: white, light gray, beige and wood-grain matched to door exteriors.

Thickness range: 0.12–0.18mm for carcass lining; edge banding 0.4–2mm for exposed edges.

Matching Edge Banding

A finished cabinet door is only as good as its edge. GONGDA produces PVC edge banding tape in exact color and texture matches to our decorative film range — ensuring seamless transitions between door face and edge. Available in 0.4mm thin-roll for automated edge banders, 1mm standard for manual application, and 2mm thick-roll for high-impact zones.

3D synchronized embossing replicates the same grain pattern on the edge surface, eliminating the "edge line" visible with plain-color banding.
