FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About PVC Film

What is the difference between PVC decorative film and PVC edge banding? +
PVC decorative film is a thin sheet applied to large panel surfaces like cabinet doors and drawer fronts. PVC edge banding is a narrower strip (typically 0.4-3mm thick) applied to the exposed edges of panels to seal and protect them. Both serve different purposes but are often used together.
How do I choose between water-based and oil-based PVC ink? +
Water-based inks are required for EU, US, and Japanese markets due to strict VOC and formaldehyde regulations. They are safer for workers and eliminate the need for expensive solvent recovery systems. Oil-based inks may still be acceptable in markets with less stringent regulations and lower budgets.
What thickness of PVC edge banding should I use for kitchen cabinets? +
For kitchen cabinets, we recommend 0.8-1.5mm PVC edge banding with PUR adhesive. This thickness provides adequate durability for daily use while maintaining a clean appearance. Premium kitchens may use 2.0mm edge banding for a more substantial look.
Is soft touch PVC film suitable for outdoor use? +
Standard soft touch PVC film is designed for indoor applications. For outdoor use, you need UV-stabilized formulations with enhanced weather resistance. Our outdoor-grade soft touch films can withstand direct sunlight for 3-5 years without significant fading or degradation.
How long does PVC decorative film last? +
Quality PVC decorative film from GONGDA typically lasts 10-15 years in indoor applications with normal use. With proper care and avoidance of abrasive cleaners, the surface can maintain its appearance for the lifespan of the furniture.
Can I get custom colors and patterns for PVC film? +
Yes, GONGDA offers custom color matching and pattern development with a minimum order of 500m² per design. We provide Pantone matching, physical sample matching, and custom embossing options. Lead times are typically 7-14 days for sample approval and 15-30 days for production.
