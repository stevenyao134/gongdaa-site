FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need before placing an order.

Are you a PVC film manufacturer or trading company?

We are a factory with our own R&D, production lines and showroom, offering both standard and fully customized PVC decorative film and edge banding.

Can I get free samples?

Yes. We keep a large stock and provide free samples via DHL, UPS or FedEx for quality checking.

What is your MOQ?

Generally 2000 meters per color; smaller quantities are accepted from stock.

Do you provide OEM/ODM service?

Yes. We customize logo, pattern, size and packaging, and protect your exclusive colors from being sold to competitors.

What are your lead times?

5–10 days if in stock; 15–20 days for production after payment.

What are your payment terms?

T/T 30% deposit and 70% before delivery, or 100% irrevocable LC at sight.

What certifications do you have?

ISO9001, ISO14001, SGS and REACH, meeting EU and US environmental standards.

Can you match my existing film color?

Yes. With 1200+ printing rollers and 300+ embossing rollers, we can color-match most references without new tooling.
